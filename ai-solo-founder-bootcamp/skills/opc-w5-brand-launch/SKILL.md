# OPC W5 Brand Launch

## Create the brand truth

Read `OFFER.md` and create `BRAND-SOT.md`: ICP, category, competitive alternative, unique value, proof available, proof unavailable, voice, visual direction, claims allowed, claims forbidden, and primary CTA.

Do not invent testimonials, logos, customer counts, savings, awards, ratings, or market leadership.

## Launch the smallest page

Produce a page brief or implement a page containing problem, specific offer, deliverables, process, exclusions, truthful proof, FAQ, and CTA. Keep the existing public URL shape if updating a live site.

Verify the real public or preview URL, mobile layout, form or payment CTA, and absence of placeholder claims. Store only the URL and dated check in the workspace.

## Exit

Mark W5 `executed` when a URL is reachable by another person. Mark `verified` only after a target customer can explain the offer and the CTA works. End with one backlog item that directly helps W7 revenue.
