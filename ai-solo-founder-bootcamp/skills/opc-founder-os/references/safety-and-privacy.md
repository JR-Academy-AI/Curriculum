# Safety and privacy

- Ask students to work with redacted business data.
- Do not upload customer lists, identity documents, tax identifiers, full bank statements, private contracts, or employer-confidential material.
- Use official current sources for Australian government, tax, company, Grant, privacy, and employment facts.
- Label the access date and exact source link. Treat old notes as potentially stale.
- Turn regulated questions into an options summary and a question pack for a CPA, lawyer, registered migration agent, financial adviser, or Grant specialist.
- Never promise revenue, funding, Grant approval, tax outcomes, employment outcomes, or investment.
