# Evidence policy

## Evidence ladder

| Level | Evidence | Allowed conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | AI output, role-play, founder preference | Draft or hypothesis only |
| E1 | Public source or competitor page with date and URL | Market or alternative observation, not customer demand |
| E2 | Real customer conversation with consent-safe notes | Customer evidence with scope and sample limits |
| E3 | Executed external action: sent message, delivered sample, received reply | The recorded action happened |
| E4 | Inspectable commercial commitment: paid invoice, processor transaction, signed purchase step | Commercial commitment for that offer and customer |

Every Evidence ID records: date, source, observation, founder interpretation, limitation, artifact affected and next action. Personal data should be minimized and references should point to restricted originals rather than copy them into shared files.

Missing evidence is `unavailable`, never zero and never an industry average supplied by AI. Test-mode transactions test the payment path only; they are not revenue. Friend payments, refunds, chargebacks and founder-funded transactions must be labelled rather than hidden.
