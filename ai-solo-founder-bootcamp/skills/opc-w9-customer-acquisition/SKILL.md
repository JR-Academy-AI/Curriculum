# OPC W9 Customer Acquisition

## Define reachable ICP

Read the verified customer and offer files. Create `ACQUISITION.md` with one segment, observable qualification criteria, ethical data sources, exclusion rules, channel hypothesis, message angle, daily volume, follow-up sequence, and seven-day success threshold.

Do not scrape or store prohibited personal data. Respect platform rules, opt-outs, privacy, and anti-spam requirements. Do not mass-send generic AI copy.

## Execute with the student

Prepare research and message drafts. Require the student to approve and send external messages. Update `SALES-PIPELINE.md` with sent, delivered when available, reply, qualified reply, meeting, proposal, won, lost, and next action.

## Review

Separate channel failure, list failure, message failure, offer failure, and follow-up failure. Change one major variable per next experiment.

## Exit

Mark `executed` after real outreach is sent. Mark `verified` only when sent volume and real replies are logged. Never mark a zero as failure without checking delivery and sufficient sample size.
