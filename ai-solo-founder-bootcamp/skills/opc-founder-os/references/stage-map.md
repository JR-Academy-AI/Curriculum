# Capability map

| Capability | Decision | Required artifact | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `$opc-offer-mvp` | What can I offer and safely deliver now? | `OFFER.md`, `MVP-DELIVERY.md`, `OFFER-TEST.md` | Offer shown to a qualified target customer and response recorded |
| `$opc-shipping-review` | What can I finish this week? | `BACKLOG.md`, `WEEKLY-REVIEW.md` | Chosen increment inspected against its Definition of Done |
| `$opc-first-dollar` | Will a qualified non-friend pay for this offer? | `SALES-PIPELINE.md`, `PAYMENT-EVIDENCE.md` | Real settled payment or honest objection evidence |
| `$opc-customer-acquisition` | Can one approved channel create qualified conversations? | `ACQUISITION-EXPERIMENT.md`, `PROSPECT-SOURCE.md` | Sent outreach and real replies measured against a pre-set rule |

The sequence can loop. New objections may route back to the offer; delivery failure may route back to scope; weak outreach evidence may require changing the list or channel rather than rewriting the business story.
