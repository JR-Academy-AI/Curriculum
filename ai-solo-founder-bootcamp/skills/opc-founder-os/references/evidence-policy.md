# Evidence policy

Use exactly three delivery states:

- `drafted`: AI or student created a plan or artifact.
- `executed`: the student performed the external action.
- `verified`: inspectable evidence exists and the required reviewer has accepted it.

Acceptable evidence includes dated interview notes, sent-message logs, public URLs, provider receipts, redacted payment records, official submission confirmations, and tutor review notes.

Never treat compliments, hypothetical purchase intent, AI role-play, generated screenshots, private local drafts, or a student's unsupported claim as verified evidence.

Store evidence references, not unnecessary sensitive data. Redact customer names, contact details, payment identifiers, tax identifiers, identity documents, contracts, and health information.
