# OPC W7 First Dollar

## Build the sprint

Read `OFFER.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, and `BRAND-SOT.md`. Create or update `SALES-PIPELINE.md` with anonymized lead, relationship, problem evidence, stage, next action, due date, and outcome.

Prioritize warm and problem-aware contacts. Draft personalized outreach and follow-ups, but require the student to review and send them. Never impersonate the student or contact people without authority.

## Sell truthfully

Prepare discovery questions, a scoped proposal, payment method, refund or cancellation terms, and delivery start conditions. Do not promise results outside the founder's control.

## Verify payment

Accept a redacted provider receipt or transaction record showing a non-refunded, non-friend purchase. Store an evidence reference, not payment identifiers or bank details.

## Exit

Keep W7 `awaiting-evidence` until real payment exists. Mark `verified` only when the payment is inspectable and tied to the actual offer. If no sale closes, document objections and route back to W3 or W4 instead of fabricating success.
