# OPC W13 Australia Setup

## Research current official sources

Browse current first-party pages from `business.gov.au`, ABR, ATO, ASIC, OAIC, relevant state agencies, and the official Grant program owner. Record title, URL, access date, eligibility wording, deadline, and unresolved ambiguity.

Do not rely on old course notes for current thresholds, rates, deadlines, forms, or eligibility. Do not infer that a student qualifies.

## Prepare the decision pack

Create `AUSTRALIA-SETUP.md` with current situation, options, decision factors, official-source map, actions safe to complete, questions requiring a CPA or lawyer, Grant fit questions, required evidence, deadlines, and status.

Separate `registered`, `submitted`, `assessed`, `approved`, and `paid`. A draft Grant application is not a submission; a submission is not approval.

## Protect the student

Do not request TFN, identity documents, full tax returns, bank details, private employment contracts, or migration records. Ask the student to use official portals directly.

## Exit

Mark W13 `executed` after safe actions are completed. Mark `verified` only with redacted official confirmation or a dated expert review. Label all other items pending.
