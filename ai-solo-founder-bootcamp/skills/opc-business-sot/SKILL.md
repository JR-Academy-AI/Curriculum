# OPC Business SoT

Build a testable business definition. Do not treat a clear document as customer validation.

## Start from evidence

1. Read the existing `BUSINESS-SOT.md` and `founder-state.json`; preserve student-written content.
2. Read `references/method-adaptation.md` before applying JTBD or Mom Test ideas.
3. Separate `observed`, `founder-knowledge`, and `assumption`. Never promote a plausible statement to fact.
4. If no customer evidence exists, say `customer validation: awaiting-evidence`.

## Build the seven-field chain

Write or revise `BUSINESS-SOT.md` with:

1. **Findable customer:** narrow enough that the founder can name where to reach five people.
2. **Customer-job hypothesis:** `When [situation], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome]`; do not mention the proposed product. Record functional, emotional, and social dimensions as hypotheses unless evidenced.
3. **Current alternative and cost:** include manual work, another product, outsourcing, or non-consumption; describe observable time, money, risk, or frustration without inventing numbers.
4. **Smallest promised outcome and deliverable:** state the change being offered and the minimum thing delivered.
5. **AI work and human accountability:** name inputs, AI output, required human approval, and prohibited automation.
6. **Six-week falsification plan:** specify evidence that could cause `continue`, `revise`, or `stop`; reserve real customer validation for W3.
7. **Not doing:** at least three customer, feature, channel, responsibility, or compliance exclusions.

Add `Facts`, `Founder Knowledge`, `Assumptions`, and `Unknowns` sections. Use `unavailable`, never zero, for missing metrics.

## Test shared understanding

Ask a peer to restate four items without viewing the page: customer, job, current alternative, and smallest promised outcome. Record material differences and revise the SoT.

Ask the AI to:

- restate the same four items;
- identify the three assumptions most likely to break the idea;
- refuse one task prohibited by `Not doing`.

## Run one real task

Choose one:

- compare three verifiable current alternatives, including doing nothing;
- draft five non-leading interview questions about past behavior and one transparent interview invitation;
- outline a one-page offer while labelling every unsupported benefit or price as an assumption.

Require the founder to correct at least one AI error. Never simulate interview responses, customer commitments, willingness to pay, or validation.

## Exit

Update `founder-state.json` with the job hypothesis, outputs, open assumptions, and next evidence action.

- `drafted`: the seven fields exist and unsupported claims are labelled.
- `executed`: peer and AI restatement tests ran, plus one real task was inspected and corrected.
- `verified`: the tutor or required reviewer confirms the Business SoT and correction evidence.

State explicitly: **Business SoT verified means the document is usable and testable; it does not mean the business idea is validated.** End with one preparation action for the AI-team stage and one customer-evidence action queued for the validation stage.
