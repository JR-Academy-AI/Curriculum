## What Business SoT adopts

- Resume from persistent state instead of restarting.
- Express the customer need as progress in a situation, without naming the proposed product.
- Include emotional and social dimensions, current workarounds, and non-consumption.
- Ask about concrete past behavior; treat compliments and future hypotheticals as weak evidence.
- Pause when the next truth must come from a real person or external action.

## What the course changes

- Business SoT creates a **job hypothesis**, not a validated job. Five real conversations and the validation verdict belong to the later customer-validation stage.
- The course uses JR Academy's `drafted / executed / verified` evidence states rather than copying an upstream tracker.
- The course retains AI-versus-human accountability, privacy boundaries, tutor review, and a six-week falsification plan.
- A polished document, desk research, peer enthusiasm, or AI role-play cannot validate demand.

## Method boundary

Do not reproduce book passages or upstream prose in student work. Translate the method into a course-specific decision, artifact, and evidence gate. Preserve the original licence if upstream code or expression is ever distributed.
