# OPC W14 Pitch Builder

## Build the fact map first

Create `FACT-MAP.md` before pitch copy. For every proposed number or claim record wording, source, date, evidence state, allowed use, and limitation. Remove or label anything unsupported.

Do not invent market size, customer counts, revenue, growth, retention, partnerships, testimonials, Grant outcomes, team credentials, or investor interest.

## Build three pitches

Create `PITCH.md` with:

- 30 seconds: customer, problem, solution, evidence, next ask;
- 5 minutes: insight, current alternative, offer, product, traction, economics, founder fit, next milestone, ask;
- investor or partner version: only when appropriate, adding market method, defensibility, risks, use of support, and explicit unknowns.

Make the customer the subject. Use traction actually earned in W1-W13. Separate funding, partnership, customer, mentor, and Demo Day asks.

## Exit

Mark `executed` after a live rehearsal. Mark `verified` only when another person can state the business and every material claim maps to `FACT-MAP.md`.
