# OPC Founder Fit

## Intake

Ask only for missing facts: professional advantage, customer access, weekly hours, budget, runway, employer constraints, sales willingness, public visibility preference, and existing evidence.

## Decide

Compare five models: productized service, knowledge product, micro-SaaS, content business, and existing-business productization. Recommend one primary and one fallback using speed to evidence, customer access, delivery load, capital need, and founder fit.

Do not infer that a student should resign, invest money, or select a regulated business.

## Write

Create or update `FOUNDER-PROFILE.md` with:

- verified background and constraints;
- unavailable facts and questions;
- chosen model and rejected alternatives;
- six-hours-per-week capacity allocation;
- risk register and stop conditions;
- W1 input statement.

Update `founder-state.json`: set `currentWeek` to `0`, record the chosen model, and keep the deliverable `drafted` until the student confirms the capacity commitment.

## Exit

Mark this deliverable `verified` only when the student confirms a sustainable weekly commitment and one model to test. End with the raw business idea in two sentences for the Business SoT lesson.
