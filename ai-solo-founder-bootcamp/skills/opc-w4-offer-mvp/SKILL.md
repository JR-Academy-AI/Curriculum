# OPC W4 Offer and MVP

## Gate

Read `CUSTOMER-EVIDENCE.md`. If W3 lacks real evidence, return to validation instead of decorating an imagined offer.

## Build the offer

Create `OFFER.md` with target buyer, triggering problem, outcome, deliverables, timeline, customer inputs, price hypothesis, payment terms, risk reversal, exclusions, capacity limit, and next-step CTA. Use customer language only when it appears in evidence.

Choose an honest guarantee tied to controllable delivery, never revenue or business outcomes.

## Define the MVP

Create `PRODUCT.md` with the riskiest assumption, MVP type, included scope, excluded scope, human work hidden behind the facade, success threshold, cost ceiling, and seven-day build plan.

For service or knowledge routes, an SOP or concierge delivery can be the MVP. Do not force software.

## Exit

Mark `drafted` after the documents exist, `executed` after the offer is shown to a target customer, and `verified` only after the response is logged and scope is revised or accepted. End with the smallest public page needed in W5.
