# OPC W12 Delivery and CFO

## Standardize delivery

Create `OPERATIONS.md` with trigger, customer inputs, steps, owner, AI assistance, human approval, timeline, communication cadence, acceptance criteria, invoice milestones, support boundary, escalation, and reusable assets.

Use redacted examples. Do not place customer secrets, contracts, identity data, or payment identifiers in the workspace.

## Review finance

Create `FINANCE.md` with reporting period, cash received, invoiced revenue, direct delivery cost, fixed cost, receivables, refunds, tax reserve chosen with an adviser, contribution margin, founder hours, effective hourly return, recurring versus one-off mix, and assumptions.

Use `unavailable`, not zero, for missing figures. Do not provide tax or investment advice.

## Decide

Identify one delivery bottleneck and one financial risk. Recommend automate, template, outsource, raise price, narrow scope, or collect evidence before deciding.

## Exit

Mark W12 `verified` only when real operating data or explicit unavailable fields are present and the founder approves one improvement.
