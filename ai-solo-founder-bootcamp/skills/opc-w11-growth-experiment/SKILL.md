# OPC W11 Growth Experiment

## Diagnose one bottleneck

Read real funnel data. If data is missing, state `unavailable` and choose an instrumentation experiment before an optimization experiment.

Append an experiment card to `EXPERIMENTS.md`: observation, bottleneck, hypothesis, one changed variable, audience, baseline, primary metric, guardrail metric, sample or duration, success threshold, stop condition, rollback, owner, and start date.

Do not select multiple variables that make the result uninterpretable. Do not claim statistical certainty from a tiny sample.

## Run and decide

Record raw results in `METRICS.md`. Compare with the pre-committed threshold and choose `continue`, `modify`, `stop`, or `insufficient evidence`. Preserve losing experiments.

## Exit

Mark W11 `executed` after the experiment runs for its planned duration. Mark `verified` only after results and decision are recorded without moving the threshold after seeing the outcome.
