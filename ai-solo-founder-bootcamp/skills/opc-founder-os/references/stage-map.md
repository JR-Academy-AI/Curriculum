# OPC stage map

| Week | Decision | Required artifact | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| W0 | Can I sustain this? | `FOUNDER-PROFILE.md` | Capacity and risk choices confirmed |
| W1 | What business am I testing? | `BUSINESS-SOT.md` | Another person and AI can restate it |
| W2 | What can AI safely execute? | `AGENT-TEAM.md` | One task run and human-checked |
| W3 | Is the problem real? | `CUSTOMER-EVIDENCE.md` | Five real conversations and verdict |
| W4 | What can I sell now? | `OFFER.md`, `PRODUCT.md` | Offer shown to a target customer |
| W5 | Where can buyers inspect it? | `BRAND-SOT.md` | Public product or service URL |
| W6 | What ships this week? | `BACKLOG.md` | One-week plan completed or reviewed |
| W7 | Will anyone pay? | `SALES-PIPELINE.md` | Real non-friend payment evidence |
| W8 | What content attracts the ICP? | `CONTENT-SYSTEM.md` | Public Chinese and English posts |
| W9 | Can outreach create pipeline? | `ACQUISITION.md` | Sent outreach and replies logged |
| W10 | Can search discover and cite us? | `SEARCH-GROWTH.md` | Live source-backed search asset |
| W11 | Which growth lever works? | `EXPERIMENTS.md` | Pre-set decision rule evaluated |
| W12 | Can one person deliver profitably? | `OPERATIONS.md`, `FINANCE.md` | Real or explicitly unavailable metrics |
| W13 | What Australian actions need experts? | `AUSTRALIA-SETUP.md` | Official sources and expert questions |
| W14 | What is true enough to pitch? | `PITCH.md`, `FACT-MAP.md` | Every claim mapped to evidence |
| W15 | Did the founder graduate? | `FOUNDER-PASSPORT.md` | Six hard gates audited |
