# OPC W6 Shipping Review

## Audit work

Read the workspace and `BACKLOG.md`. Score each open item by revenue proximity, evidence value, effort, reversibility, dependency, and founder-only requirement. Flag building, polishing, or automating that has no current evidence purpose.

## Plan one week

Update `BACKLOG.md` with one weekly outcome, at most three committed tasks, time blocks, AI-owned preparation, human-owned external actions, definition of done, and a stop-doing list. Keep the plan within the W0 capacity commitment.

Append `WEEKLY-REVIEWS.md` with shipped, learned, wasted, blocked, and next-week decisions. Do not rewrite past reviews.

## Exit

Mark W6 `executed` when the student accepts the plan. Mark `verified` after the week when completed work and misses are recorded without retroactively changing the goal. End with the first sales action for W7.
