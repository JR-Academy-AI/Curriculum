# OPC W15 Graduation Auditor

## Audit six gates

Create `GRADUATION-EVIDENCE.md` with one row for each course gate:

1. real payment;
2. five Chinese and five English public content links;
3. ABN registration or existing company evidence;
4. at least one official Grant submission;
5. accessible product or service URL;
6. Demo Day attendance.

For each record status, redacted evidence reference, date, reviewer, limitation, and remediation. Never accept a promise, draft, local file, AI role-play, or unreviewed screenshot as verified.

## Produce the passport

Create `FOUNDER-PASSPORT.md`: founder thesis, customer, offer, live asset, verified traction, experiments, operating system, Australian setup status, key lessons, current risks, next 90-day focus, and public/private fields.

Exclude sensitive customer, payment, identity, tax, and employment data. Obtain student consent before creating a public case study.

## Decide

Return `verified graduate`, `completion with missing gates`, or `not yet auditable`. Do not soften the result. Give one remediation action per missing gate.

## Exit

Mark W15 verified only after the authorized tutor accepts all six evidence rows. Demo Day participation alone is not graduation.
